# Product Context

## Problem

Security systems can fail silently. A camera may be unplugged, a recorder may stop, a router may lose power, or the property may lose its outside connection. Cloud cameras are especially vulnerable to the paradox that they cannot report an outage after their communication path disappears.

## Product promise

When the internet, monitoring agent, camera, recorder, or selected critical device stops protecting the property, the owner should learn about it remotely and quickly.

## Initial customer

- rural homeowners;
- owners of camps, second homes, workshops, garages, barns, and unattended properties;
- small businesses without dedicated IT staff;
- users of Starlink, cable, DSL, fixed wireless, or cellular internet;
- people already using cameras, DVRs/NVRs, hubs, switches, and alarms.

## Differentiation

The product is not a general-purpose network scanner. It monitors the infrastructure that enables property protection and translates technical evidence into a plain-English incident timeline.

The primary differentiators are:

- critical-device watchlists;
- cloud detection of property silence;
- local evidence preserved through outages;
- dependency-aware incident correlation;
- possible-disconnection and sequential-failure pattern detection;
- camera/recorder health layers;
- push/email/SMS escalation;
- cautious, evidence-based language.

## Product principles

1. Alert reliability beats feature count.
2. One useful incident is better than an alert storm.
3. Evidence and uncertainty must remain distinguishable.
4. The system must fail visibly, not pretend monitoring is active.
5. LAN credentials and detailed local data stay local unless clearly required.
6. The customer should not need to become a network administrator.
